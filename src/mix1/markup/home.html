03 / AI

Two years building with Claude API, MCP, and agentic systems as direct collaborators — not a slide in a strategy deck. Vinyl Crate is the proof: an AI-native peer-to-peer vinyl marketplace in React Native, with Stripe Connect, that saved $250K in expected dev cost.

The job is still judgment. Tools compound when the org has delivery systems, research standards, and people willing to throw the average draft away. Most teams are still prompting by hand; the leap is workflows you can reuse, then agents on the jobs that should run without a meeting.

  • L1Chats. People prompt by hand. Helpful, but nothing compounds.
  • L3Workflows. A task done the same good way every time, with a human reviewing.
  • L5Agents. Whole jobs run end to end, while people stay on the calls that matter.

Hands-on enough to know where models fall short — and to talk you out of the AI idea that will embarrass you in six months.

The tools are everywhere. The judgment isn’t.

AI is a new layer of expression, not a replacement for taste. I use it to move faster on infrastructure and prototypes — then keep the last ten percent: the call about what ships.
