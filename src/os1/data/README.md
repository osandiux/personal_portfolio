# Welcome to gucduck

You made it. No need to duck and cover. This is Chris’s little corner of the internet and you are invited to waddle through it.

## What is this place

gucduck is a personal website that looks and feels like macOS, the operating system on Apple computers. Everything is interactive. Click. Drag. Open. Waddle.

## How to get around

### The desktop

The desktop icons are little highlights and random treasures from my life. Projects. Experiments. Stories. Each one opens something worth poking around.

### The dock

On desktop, the dock lives at the bottom of the screen. On mobile, it shifts to the left side.

This is where the main apps live.

Finder lets you browse files.
Notes holds written thoughts.
Photos shows my photography.
Music plays tracks.
Terminal runs commands.
Apps opens the full launcher with even more things like Photo Booth and other surprises.

### The top bar

Click gucduck on the left to open the About window. Over on the right sit the Seattle weather, Control Center, and the date and time. Click the date and time for Notification Center, and pop open Control Center to flip on Hand Gesture Mode.

## Tips for exploring

* Move and resize windows however you like.
* The little red, yellow, and green buttons close, minimize, or expand windows.
* When a window is minimized it rests in the dock. Click the icon again to bring it back.
* Try opening multiple apps at once and rearranging the desktop.

## Hand Gesture Mode

Yes this is real.

Open Control Center in the top bar and flip on Hand Gesture Mode to control the desktop with your camera. Pinch and hold to move things. Open your palm to open or close items. It feels a little like magic. Or like you are conducting an orchestra of ducks.
